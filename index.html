The Work · 12 Weeks

You can run in loops until you're exhausted enough.

Or you can take a hand. Twelve weeks, one to one or in a circle, for the moment the overthinking stops and your body is allowed to rest into the now.

Phase 01 · Wk 1–4

Admit the truth you've been hiding.

Out loud, in front of someone who doesn't flinch. Most of what you fear is the saying, not the thing.

Phase 02 · Wk 5–8

Let yourself fall.

Your patterns don't live in your understanding. They live in your jaw, your shoulders, your breath. This is where they leave the body.

Phase 03 · Wk 9–12

Keep your head up.

The real measure of accountability is what happens when no one is watching.

Twelve weeks is a beginning, not a deadline. If you need more time or more individual care, we extend. Integration has its own pace.

Is this for you

You already feel it, don't you.

This work is for you if you recognise yourself here:

  • You're the one who holds everyone, and quietly wonders who holds you.
  • You keep meeting the same pattern in new disguises: in love, in work, in your own body.
  • You can say exactly what's wrong, and still can't seem to move it.
  • You're tired. Not sleepy. Tired of pretending.
  • You don't want another framework. You want to come home to yourself.
